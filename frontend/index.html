The red fox (Vulpes vulpes) is the largest of the true foxes,
and the most widely distributed member of the order Carnivora.
It is present across the entire Northern Hemisphere,
including most of North America, Europe and Asia, as well as parts of North Africa.
Its range has expanded alongside human settlement,
with the species having been introduced to Australia,
where it preys on native small and medium-sized rodents and marsupials.
The red fox is listed as of least concern on the IUCN Red List.
Because of its impact on native species,
it is included on the list of the "world's 100 worst invasive species".
